Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I block apps during a focus session?
    Buzee uses Apple's Screen Time framework to block apps you choose while a session is running. The first time you start a session, you'll be asked to grant Screen Time permission — this is required for app blocking to work.
  • How do rewards work?
    Completing a focus session earns you a reward pull, unlocking mascots and collectibles across different rarity tiers.
  • How do streaks work?
    Your streak increases each day you complete at least one focus session, and resets if a day is missed.
  • The app isn't blocking the apps I selected — what should I do?
    Make sure Screen Time permission is granted in your iPhone's Settings app under Screen Time → Buzee. If the issue continues, email us at buzeeapp@gmail.com with your device model and iOS version.
